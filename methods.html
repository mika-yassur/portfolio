Midterm review Colloquium II Explore, Explain, Propose

Designed entirely in Google Sheets, this presentation explored the implications of the grid in cartography, colonial schemes of measurement, and AI image processing.

This piece incorporates archival imagery, maps traced and modelled in QGIS and Rhino, data from scientific soil testing sites, data visualizations based on oral testimonies, and Blender models.
